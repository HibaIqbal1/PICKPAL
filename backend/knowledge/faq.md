# PickPal Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## General Questions

### Q: What is PickPal?
**A**: PickPal is a hyperlocal quick-commerce marketplace and on-demand service delivery platform connecting customers with local shop vendors, verified technicians, and delivery partners for rapid sub-30 minute shopping errands, doorstep repairs, and salon queue tokens.

### Q: Which services can come to my home?
**A**: Home Services include Electrician, Plumber, Carpenter, Painter, AC Repair, Appliance Repair, Cleaning, Gardening, custom Grocery/Errand shopping, and Parcel Pick & Drop. Verified technicians travel directly to your location.

### Q: How do shop visit services work?
**A**: For shop visit services (Salon, Beautician, Barber, Spa, Laundry, Tailoring, Clinics), you can either book a scheduled appointment slot or request a digital walk-in queue token from your mobile phone.

### Q: How does salon token booking work?
**A**: When you book a salon token, PickPal issues a digital ticket with a unique token number (e.g. `Q-18`). The app tracks the current serving token in real-time, calculates how many people are ahead of you, and estimates your wait time (20 minutes per person ahead). You arrive at the salon when your turn approaches and scan your OTP/QR code.

### Q: How do I track my order or worker?
**A**: Open the **Track** screen in the PickPal app. You will see an interactive Leaflet map displaying your worker's live location, route navigation, turn-by-turn status updates, and dynamic estimated arrival time.

### Q: How do I book a custom errand or grocery run?
**A**: Click **+ New Request** on the home dashboard. Enter the items you need, set your budget limit (`budgetCap`), choose your address, add optional voice or image notes, and submit. Nearby online delivery partners will bid or claim your request.

### Q: How do I cancel a booking or token?
**A**: You can cancel an active errand request in `searching_worker` or `worker_assigned` status via the order details view. For salon tokens, open your digital ticket and click **Cancel Ticket** to release your spot in line.

### Q: What payment methods are supported on PickPal?
**A**: PickPal supports payments via the PickPal Digital Wallet, UPI apps (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit Cards, QR Code scans, and Net Banking.

### Q: How does the Emergency SOS button work?
**A**: If you ever feel unsafe during an order, click the Emergency SOS button. PickPal instantly broadcasts your live GPS coordinates, order ID, and user details to platform control room administrators for immediate emergency assistance.

### Q: How do workers get verified on PickPal?
**A**: Workers must submit government identification (Aadhaar card front/back, PAN card), a clear live selfie, address details, and skills. Platform admins audit these documents before approving the worker's account to accept jobs.
